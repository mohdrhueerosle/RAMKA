Our Story

The Making of RAMKA

The Beginning
A Boy from Sungai Petani
Rhuee was born and raised in Sungai Petani, Kedah. At 13, he was selected to attend Malay College Kuala Kangsar (MCKK) — one of Malaysia's most prestigious boarding schools — where he served as President of the Student Union and received the Leadership Award. Those years shaped the way he thinks, leads, and carries responsibility.
The Date That Changed Everything
8 April — Born, Married, and Bound Together
Rhuee and Afiqah share the same birthday — 8 April 1990. On 8 April 2015, after returning to Malaysia, they chose their shared birthday as their Nikah date. Not a coincidence — a choice. A date that now carries three meanings: two lives, one beginning.
Rhuee & Afiqah · Nikah Day 8 April 2015 💍
"We were born on the same day. So we chose to begin our life together on that same day."
Then They Arrived
The Day Everything Changed — Three Times
Maryam came first, on 29 December 2015. Then Khadeeja, on 24 June 2018. Then Aeesya, on 15 January 2020. Three different days. The same arms. The same look — quiet, overwhelmed, grateful. Three times, Rhuee became a father again.

The PETRONAS Years
RAPID, Pengerang & The Megaproject
Rhuee spent years at PETRONAS / PRPC as Manager, Contract Management CFA at Petronas Refinery and Petrochemical Corporation in Pengerang, Johor. He witnessed empty land transform into one of the world's largest integrated refinery and petrochemical complexes. He was involved in complex tariff structures (Capacity Charge, Fixed O&M, Variable O&M, Fuel Pass-through), negotiations, financial modelling, and presenting findings to the EVP Downstream.

He once said he wanted to write about that journey someday. The scale of what he saw — and built — deserved to be recorded.
RAPID / PRPC Contract Management Tariff Structures Hazardous Waste / RRF / ETP Presented to EVP Downstream
The Move
Qatar — A New Chapter
In 2024, Rhuee accepted an offer from QatarEnergy as Senior Economic Analyst. He moved first, staying at La Cigale Hotel while preparing everything — visas, schools, accommodation — for the family to follow. The family eventually settled in Ezdan Village 11, Al Wakrah.

The migration wasn't just a career move. It was a complete family transition — full of uncertainty, adjustment, exhaustion, and eventually, a new home.
QatarEnergy Senior Economic Analyst Al Wakrah, Doha Arrived 2024
Who Rhuee Is
The Man Behind RAMKA
Analytical by nature, sentimental at heart. He thinks in systems — budgets, routes, models, optimizations — but he remembers emotional details: the empty land at Pengerang, his daughters growing older, feeding Rimau the cat outside his house who sleeps on a chair but isn't his.

He loves candid photography with his Fujifilm X-T30 II (with prime lenses — 90mm f/2, 35mm f/2, 16mm f/2.8). He drinks karak and Kashmiri tea. He drives a Toyota Harrier. He wants to memorize the Quran, learn Arabic, and pray Maghrib at the mosque more often.

He thinks simultaneously at the strategic level, the logistical level, and the emotional level — always.
"My daughters love me very much."
